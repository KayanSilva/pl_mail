**Important mail alerts and morning digest.** Incoming high-priority messages (government, bills,
and family from Google Contacts) trigger immediate Telegram alerts during active hours (06:00 to
20:00) and are aggregated into a single-line JSON digest (`app:important:digest`) for daily morning
briefings. Nighttime arrivals are withheld from immediate alert and prioritized in the digest.
